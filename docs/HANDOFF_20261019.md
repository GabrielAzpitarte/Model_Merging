## What happens automatically

1. **S1 (Llama-3.1-8B, 8 experts):** 405 dev evaluations (about 4 minutes each), then the dev selection is frozen
   into `configs/llm/frozen_S1.json` and committed. After that it runs:
   * test and fresh evaluations of the frozen configurations, plus 15 matched-config shuffles;
   * noise-floor replicates and Iso-C;
   * gradient-preference diagnostics;
   * the tail edit (CPU prep, then 18 jobs).
2. **S2 (Llama-3.2-3B, 8 experts):** own-dev replication with the same steps, plus a transfer of S1's frozen
   configurations.
3. **S3 (Llama-3.2-3B, 20 experts):** replication. **S4 (Llama-3.1-8B, 20 experts):** optional, transfer only,
   lowest priority.
4. **Uncertainty gate** (`experiments/uncert/gate.py`): enqueued only if the diagnostic in
   `results/uncert/diagnostic_summary.json` passes its pre-registered criterion. Otherwise it is recorded as "not run".
5. **At the end:**
   * `experiments/llm/analyze.py` writes `results/llm/summary/SUMMARY.md` and the figures in
     `results/llm/summary/figures/`;
   * the results are committed locally and backed up to `/shared/ssd_30T/azpitarte_condmerge_backup`;
   * `ALL_DONE` is written, and the workers exit.

Expected timing: S1 frozen around 13:00; main results (S1-S3 test/fresh/tail) around 17:00; optional S4 around 21:00.

## Results already in (completed and verified 2026-10-19)

* **CLIP reproduction** (`results/repro/`). Every frozen B/32 test configuration rerun so far (uniform, literal
  kappa, kappa_rev, the 5 shuffles) reproduces exactly, with 100 % per-image agreement. The full-FT RegMean
  strength-sweep and LoRA RegMean frozen tests reproduce to within 0.02 pt.
* **Collapse audit** (`results/inverse/`). The LoRA RegMean "4.1 %" collapses are fp16 overflow: in fp32,
  plain RegMean is within ~0.3 pt of the kappa cap. The full-FT n = 16 degradation is statistical (65 in fp16 and in
  fp32, vs 88 with the cap).
* **LLM spectra** (`results/llm/spectral/`):
  * the computation is exact (independent algorithms agree to ~1e-12);
  * in q_proj and o_proj, sigma_min of every expert matrix lies 20-1000x below the BF16 resolution floor of the
    stored base, and all of the across-expert kappa variation comes from sigma_min;
  * v_proj and k_proj are resolved and nearly identical across experts.
