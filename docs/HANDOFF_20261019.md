## What happens automatically

1. **S1 (Llama-3.1-8B, 8 experts):** 486 dev evaluations (about 4.2 minutes each on 4 cards). The dev selection is
   then frozen into `configs/llm/frozen_S1.json` and committed. After that it runs:
   * test and fresh evaluations of the frozen configurations;
   * 15 matched shuffles at kappa|tuned, plus 10 at kappa|alpha1;
   * noise-floor replicates and Iso-C;
   * gradient-preference diagnostics;
   * the tail edit (CPU prep, then 18 jobs).
2. **S2 (Llama-3.2-3B, 8 experts):** own-dev replication with the same steps (968 dev evaluations), plus a transfer
   of S1's frozen configurations.
3. **S3 (Llama-3.2-3B, 20 experts):** replication (1089 dev evaluations, all on the 3B card).
   **S4 (Llama-3.1-8B, 20 experts):** optional, transfer only, lowest priority.
4. **Uncertainty gate** (`experiments/uncert/gate.py`): enqueued only if the diagnostic in
   `results/uncert/diagnostic_summary.json` passes its pre-registered criterion. Otherwise it is recorded as "not run".
5. **At the end:**
   * `experiments/llm/analyze.py` writes `results/llm/summary/SUMMARY.md`, the figures in
     `results/llm/summary/figures/`, and the cross-study table `docs/REPORT_03_tables.md`;
   * the results are committed locally and backed up to `/shared/ssd_30T/azpitarte_condmerge_backup`;
   * `ALL_DONE` is written, the workers exit, and the watcher re-runs the analysis if its jobs finished later.
